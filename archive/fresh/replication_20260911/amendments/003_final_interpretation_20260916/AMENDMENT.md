# Amendment 003: final interpretation of saved stopped-study results

This supplement performs no fitting or path sampling. It preserves the original source, worker manifests, raw archives, generated report and figures. It adds a separately versioned final interpretation and numerical adjudication from existing results, following the user's request to stop and analyze and the 16 September code-path review.

The supplementary analyses are descriptive and post hoc, not new confirmatory tests. Derivative contrasts (full versus half, half versus quarter, full versus quarter) and original versus doubled solver steps are paired by the three saved Monte Carlo seeds and exact fold/phase/lag/source/target/horizon keys. For achieved-gap normalization, report both all-valid positive-gap cells and the more conservative cells with all achieved gaps at least 0.10. Requested-gap normalization is also retained. Report mean differences, root mean squared differences, Monte Carlo variability and paired standard errors; a two-standard-error exceedance fraction is a diagnostic, not a p-value, multiplicity-adjusted decision or equivalence test. Summarize horizon 1 separately from all horizons. No reliability threshold is selected from atlas scores.

Strict-gap atlas sensitivity uses the stored strict arrays and ordinary equal-recording/equal-seed means, propagating any NaN. Report remaining eligible coverage and compare strict, primary floor-normalized and SBTG scores on the identical surviving mask. Empty masks remain unevaluated. No available-event mean is substituted. These scores were not produced by the original external-score function.

Particle error is pooled using saved qualified-reference cell counts, alongside mean error across the original lag/phase strata. Computation is summarized from per-run receipts, never summed from target-expanded rows. Counts of significant lag cells and distinct directed pairs are distinguished. Final report figures use corrected subplot spacing and explicit descriptions; original figures remain intact. Identical-clamp zero controls are described as paired-arm symmetry checks, correcting the original report's contradictory noise-floor sentence.

The final artifact manifest records supplementary script and output hashes. Extended-training results remain excluded. Historical provenance, pooled clean vocabulary, temporal GroupNorm, soft finite contrasts, conditional fixed-model uncertainty and unresolved clean-model convergence are carried into the final interpretation.
