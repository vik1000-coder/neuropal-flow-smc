## Corrections and limitations to carry into the final report

1. `raw` means the MAT `norm_traces` field without additional deconvolution, not raw camera fluorescence. Left/right and designated dorsal/ventral traces are averaged into cell classes; this is not a single-neuron connectome. Variant traces are tail-aligned to their shortest length inside collect_worm_trace; the per-worm class matrix then truncates columns to its shortest time length.
2. Clean cohort selection first considers both head strains (28 candidate recordings), Cook-node membership and 90% coverage, then complete-case recordings (20: 17 OH16230 + 3 OH15500). It applies a per-coordinate finite-frame quality check across those 20 and drops ADA/AIN, then selects the 17 OH16230 recordings. The 54-class vocabulary is thus pooled-data-defined, not independently selected within each fold or exclusively from OH16230. No donor-trace replacement is used for clean recordings. Do not describe the entire preprocessing as training-only.
3. The combined loader applies a pooled per-neuron centering and common global scale before the training-only per-neuron scaler. Algebraically that affine transformation cancels under a subsequent ordinary training-only standardization, except clipping/roundoff. Reconstructing retained clean traces from the original MAT and comparing all five folds gave maximum absolute discrepancy 3.815e-6. Historical prepared data are already standardized and imputed; inherited donor dependence is not fixed by the fold scaler.
4. Training history construction forward-fills internal missing values, leaves leading missing history values missing, and rejects unobserved targets. Sampler causal_fill also backfills leading missing frames with the first observation. Every coordinate has some initial missing data, but no registered response episode/history (including timing-aligned lags) overlaps the backfilled leading frames. Training and sampling missing-value policies are not identical.
5. The actual model uses the legacy `tcn`, not `full_history_tcn`. For L80 its local convolutional receptive field is 31 frames; GroupNorm(1,width) spans time and channels, giving older frames an indirect effect through normalization. A deterministic gradient check of an existing checkpoint confirms older-frame influence; replacing those norms with Identity in a temporary CPU copy eliminates the older-frame gradient. It is inaccurate to claim either full 80-frame local temporal coverage or complete blindness beyond 31 frames. No future outside the supplied history is used.
6. Models learn joint one-step residuals with independent-coupling conditional flow-matching MSE. They are not trained on atlas edges, multiple-step lag responses, or a density score. Checkpoint selection uses a fixed-random-number validation flow-matching loss. Clean history jitter perturbs neural inputs only; the original residual label is unchanged. It is a regularizer, not a physiological intervention.
7. Nuisance projection, clamp quantiles and event thresholds use every non-test recording (including validation). This matches the inherited method but is different from the model's training-only scaler/optimizer data. Model training uses histories through t-1 to predict x_t, including stimulus through t-1.
8. Repair length is lag+4; the first four generated repair frames constitute the source window and later frames lead up to the forecast cut. The source is omitted from the anchor during those four frames and anchored again during the lag gap. Anchor rank is 12 and strength .25, with Gaussian source bandwidth max(.25*max(IQR,.20),.10). The anchor is a soft low-rank population constraint, not fixing every other neuron. No factual neural target beyond the cut is used by the sampler.
9. Progressive bridge weights contain both the change in provisional source-clamp energy and increments in its strength. These potentials telescope to the same terminal source clamp as direct importance sampling. Resampling and finite populations still require empirical accuracy checks. Candidate ESS is divided by repair branching factor for the displayed equivalent ESS; it is not the count of independent trajectories. Root ancestry is tracked separately.
10. Low/high simulations share base random numbers and resampling offsets. All identical-clamp controls are exactly zero in both cohorts. This establishes coupled-arm symmetry, not an independent-arm noise floor or general sampler accuracy. Earlier generic report text about nonzero zero-query MSE must be qualified accordingly.
11. Gap normalization is a finite contrast, not automatic differentiation of the flow. The primary normalization floors abs(achieved gap) at .10 and does not exclude invalid episodes. Strong-support results and signed-gap sensitivity must be distinguished. The strict arrays use ordinary means after invalid values become NaN, so an invalid event propagates to the corresponding phase/recording cell; they are a conservative complete-case sensitivity, not an available-event mean. The original external-score function does not yet score the stored strict arrays; the final report must not imply those strict comparisons have already been evaluated. Add an explicitly labeled supplementary table if reporting them.
12. Derivative and solver tables retain MC means/SD and raw evidence; final interpretation must compare step-size changes to MC variation. Stable atlas AUROC alone does not certify these properties. Incomplete extended fits (9/15) and archives (34/60) cannot be substituted into a complete ensemble.
