# Prospective computational replication — 11 September 2026

This is a fresh computational replication on existing recordings, not independent biological replication. No pretrained flow checkpoint or previous response matrix is used. Published SBTG matrices are a frozen comparator, not training targets. All work is isolated here. The source and raw/reference inputs are copied and hashed before fitting.

## Questions and decision rules

1. Does retraining the fixed previously selected flow recover its predictive performance and reference correspondence? Use all five held-out recording folds and all three generator seeds (1701, 2903, 4307); do not select the best seed, lag, channel, or model by atlas results.
2. Does progressive bridge SMC approximate the intended repaired path law? First test an analytic multivariate Gaussian path oracle including nonzero population anchoring, averaged-source clamps, multiple lags, and horizons. Then compare independent MC seeds and increasing particle counts against independent large direct-importance banks under each learned generator. A high-particle run is only a reference when its independent repeat agrees and its ESS/support passes; otherwise mark unresolved.
3. Does the matrix beat the published SBTG matrix? Primary historical reproduction uses nominal source lag 1, forecast horizon 1, state-average endpoint-mean response; compare AUROC on Randi wild type and Cook structural/chemical/gap on exactly the same off-diagonal edge masks. Record AUPRC, prevalence, within-source macro metrics, and source-block paired bootstrap intervals. Compare all three seeds and their arithmetic ensemble, never the selected best seed. Four primary historical AUROC deltas form one Holm family. A positive point estimate alone is not superiority; a positive simultaneous/adjusted lower confidence bound is required. Other cohorts, masks, channels, and timing comparisons are separately labeled sensitivities.
4. Are lag profiles reproducible, supported, and distinguishable from numerical noise? Recompute full lag and forecast grids, seed and MC agreement, fixed across-lag support, baseline effects, onset-minus-matched-baseline effects, and whole-recording lag contrasts. Exact joint recording sign-flip max-T covers the prespecified baseline/onset-minus-baseline mean/log-SD effect and lag-contrast families; no post-screen p-values. Historical recording rows contain copied data and are not independent animals: their inference is descriptive. Clean-cohort conditional sign-flip inference still assumes joint symmetry and does not include full refit uncertainty. No physical-delay or causal claim is permitted.
5. Is the gap-normalized contrast a derivative approximation? At atlas-blind selected sources/episodes, repeat symmetric clamp contrasts at 1, 1/2, and 1/4 of the training IQR separation with fixed clamp bandwidth, using N512 and three independent MC seeds. Compare raw contrast, achieved gap, requested gap, and both normalizations. The absolute 0.10 denominator floor is used only for exact historical reproduction. Small-gap ratios cannot be stabilized artificially by that floor and called derivatives. A bandwidth floor of 0.10 in the original estimator is retained and disclosed: even a converged narrow contrast differentiates a softened model-conditional law, not a physical intervention.

## Aggregation and support

Keep raw source-major arrays; independently convert once to target-row/source-column. Reproduce original raw contrast divided by max(abs(achieved gap), .10), then average events within phase and phases within recording, and average seeds within recording. Alongside this reproduction, report signed-gap-only estimates with unsupported/reversed/sub-.10 gaps excluded; never hide coverage loss or count dropped cells as zeros. Source compatibility valid flag, pre-resampling ESS/maximum weight, achieved displacement, distinct-root ancestry, and independent MC agreement are separate diagnostics.

Strong fixed support requires each source to have at least .80 valid episode fraction and .80 genealogy-qualified episode fraction at every lag; genealogy-qualified means at least .20 distinct-root fraction in both arms. A .50 sensitivity is reported separately. Fix common masks across compared methods and lags. Report support counts and effective denominators for each reference. Lag contrasts must use paired complete recordings on a fixed support mask, not changing populations at different lags.

Full biological replication and exhaustive bootstrap refitting of the complete selection procedure are not claimed. This fixed-model computational replication estimates seed/partition/Monte Carlo stability and conditional recording variation, not uncertainty over independent new animals.
