# NeuroPAL figure guide

The clarity-first collection now contains **22 standalone figures: 20 new views and the two preserved prototypes**.

Start with the [visual gallery](results/figure_story_20260831/GALLERY.md), or use the [figure index](results/figure_story_20260831/README.md) for individual PNGs, editable SVGs, captions, and source-data CSVs.

The reading sequence covers recordings, sampling, Cook/Randi correspondence, neuromodulator lags, mean effects, distributional effects, and observed stimulus responses. Separate companion views retain every planned pathway and all four selected modeled onset follow-ups.

These are reorganized saved results, not new training or inference. Statistical evidence, exploratory status, and missing controls remain explicit. The [original technical figure bundle](results/figure_atlas_20260831/README.md) and [two prototypes](results/figure_redesign_prototypes_20260831/README.md) are unchanged.
