How to read the uncertainty

Uncertainty in the mean effect

The shaded 95% interval comes from resampling the 17 worms. It holds the trained models fixed and is not corrected for exploring different cells.

Variation between worms

Individual lines and dots show how the model effect varies across the same 17 worms. Seeds, events, and particles are not additional animals.

Spread of possible outcomes

Low/high prediction distributions show generated outcomes for a single saved worm, event, model seed and sampling repeat. Their width is not a confidence interval for an effect.

Sampling noise and support

Finite-particle controls test numerical distinguishability. Support and ancestry checks still apply, even for a narrow interval or small p-value.
