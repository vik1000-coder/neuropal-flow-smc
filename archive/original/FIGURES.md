# Scientific figures

The current figure collection is [NeuroPAL: data, methods, and results in figures](results/figure_atlas_20260831/README.md).

It contains editable SVGs, high-resolution PNGs, plotted-data CSVs, source hashes, and reproduction scripts for:

- the 54-class data, historical80 provenance, and actual stimulus schedules;
- conditional-flow learning and progressive-bridge repaired-path effects;
- Cook/Randi correspondence and Bentley neuromodulator lag alignment;
- corrected mean-effect candidates, unresolved lags, distributional reruns, and sampling controls;
- observed onset responses, sensory/interneuron/motor coverage, and predictive calibration.

Start with [F02: how the method works](results/figure_atlas_20260831/figures/f02_sampling_and_effect_definitions.png), then [F03: external comparisons](results/figure_atlas_20260831/figures/f03_cook_randi_correspondence.png) and [F06: statistical evidence](results/figure_atlas_20260831/figures/f06_existing_family_and_candidate_evidence.png).

[Questions and evidence](results/figure_atlas_20260831/QUESTIONS_AND_EVIDENCE.md) identifies concrete mean, spread, distributional, stimulus, and lag hypotheses without conflating significance with magnitude or experimental causality. [Validation](results/figure_atlas_20260831/VALIDATION.md) records the checks.

Dashboard path, launch command, data layers, definitions, and maintenance details are in [DASHBOARD.md](DASHBOARD.md). The figures are separate static artifacts and do not change the dashboard or its frozen scientific data.
