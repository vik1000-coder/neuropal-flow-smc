# Generator tradeoffs: supplementary controlled benchmark

13 September 2026. New, exploratory synthetic experiment, separate from the ongoing NeuroPAL replication. Original query_ood_robustness result tables/checkpoints are absent. This does not reconstruct those results and does not establish a biological winner.

## Question and fixed design

Compare generators on identical training data, held-out histories, true conditional distributions, and soft-event queries. Hold the query estimator fixed for the primary generator comparison. Separately quantify its Monte Carlo uncertainty; do not interpret sampler error as generator error.

- Four known continuous laws: linear correlated Gaussian; nonlinear heteroskedastic correlated Gaussian; history-dependent bimodal mixture; nonlinear correlated Student-t innovations (df=4). All use three history features and three output coordinates. These are controlled conditional laws, not worm trajectories or high-dimensional calcium recordings.
- Three independent data seeds (101, 202, 303), two independent neural initialization/training seeds (1701, 2903). Deterministic baseline fits occur once per dataset and are not duplicated as independent evidence.
- Training histories uniform on [-1,1]^3, 1024 training cases, 256 independent validation cases. Standardization uses training data only. There are 24 paired evaluation histories in each of: central supported, near-boundary supported, and extrapolated first-coordinate histories (2 to 3). Regions are defined before fitting; no fitted-score selection of queries.
- Nine families: ridge/full residual Gaussian; exact RBF Gaussian-process regression with Gaussian observation noise and independent output dimensions; neural diagonal Gaussian; neural rank-2 Gaussian; neural rank-2 Student-t; autoregressive MDN-4; output-autoregressive Transformer MDN-4; flow matching; EDM diffusion. Transformer uses attention across output coordinates and all three history covariates, not a long-history Transformer encoder. GP is one explicit kernel/likelihood baseline, not the entire GP family.
- Neural width32/two-layer models; Transformer width32/two attention layers/four heads/64 feedforward. Flow/EDM24 Heun steps. AdamW LR.001, weight decay.0001, batch128, gradient clip1. Native training objectives; common validation energy criterion every10 epochs using fixed64 sample banks over128 validation rows. Up to200 epochs, patience40 epochs; automatically extend to400 if the best checkpoint is within40 epochs of200. Flag any boundary-best or nonconverged final fit. No tuning on test scores. Same budgets do not guarantee equal optimization or equal compute.
- Ridge alpha selected from .1,1,10. GP length scale .5,1,2 and observation variance .05,.2,.5 in standardized output units; select by the same validation energy score. Exact GP uses all1024 cases with fixed grid, no inducing subset. Covariances are diagonal across output dimensions, which limits joint conditional effects. Persist hyperparameters, counts, losses and timing.

Predictive metrics per history: expected energy score using256 model samples and256 independent oracle outcomes; conditional-mean error; marginal90% coverage/width; upper-tail Brier score on source-output event using the true conditional95th percentile, plus tail-probability error. Same oracle outcomes across all families. Finite-bank quantile and mean uncertainty remains; these are conditional diagnostics, not exact population numbers.
2. Generator-specific conditional effect: at fixed history centers [0,0,0], [.9,0,0], [2.5,0,0], use identical Gaussian soft observation potentials on output0. Common arm centers are training-output mean +/- .5 SD; rare arm centers are mean+2.5 SD and mean+3.5 SD; bandwidth .4 training-output SD. Statistic is output1. Keep actual oracle event support/probability; 'rare' labels describe the requested query, not guaranteed rarity under every history/model. These are soft conditioning contrasts, not interventions or the progressive repaired lag path law.
3. Use three independent direct-importance banks at4096 draws for model-specific effects and65536 draws for oracle truth. Escalate model banks to16384 then65536 if minimum arm ESS<200 or repeat effect SE>.025 output units. Oracle banks escalate to262144 if the same gates fail. Record all attempts; unresolved cells remain unresolved. Qualified model-reference mean minus qualified oracle-reference mean estimates generator error. No division by an achieved gap. Compare small direct banks N128/512, three repeats, against the independent reference to characterize sampling error separately.
4. Finite-difference predictive-mean derivative with respect to history coordinate0 at three fixed centers, symmetric steps .2,.1,.05,2048 samples/arm, three repeats. Common random numbers within each +/- pair. Compare with closed-form derivative of the true mean; report all output coordinates and MC SE. Discrete mixture selections can make pathwise coupling noisy. This is a predictive-mean derivative only, not the original repaired-effect derivative or physical delay.
