# Collections and scientific status

## Cohorts

- `historical80`: 20 prepared traces, 80 neuron classes; inherited head/tail
  pseudo-pairing and donor-trace copying. Computational reproduction only.
- Early pooled54: 20 retained head recordings, includes two strains. Some older
  clock/stimulus interpretations are superseded.
- `clean54`: 17 OH16230 head recordings, native 4 Hz, no donor copying. The 54-class
  vocabulary was selected using pooled head coverage before the strain restriction;
  it is not a wholly fold-nested or exclusively OH16230-defined vocabulary.
- Bridge54: historical-to-current representation sensitivity in the older tournament.

## Original program

The preserved [experiment index](../archive/original/EXPERIMENT_INDEX.md) maps E01–E32.
The principal NeuroPAL sequence is E15–E32: generator screening; repaired responses;
sampler comparison; predictive/self-history ceilings; distributed/higher-order lags;
stimulus correction; distributional contrasts; corrected four-sampler comparison;
54 atlas; initial negative80 result; complete-family inference; distribution-shape
and neuron-class audits; optimized80 training and atlas.

**Supersession:** the actual epochs are butanone, pentanedione and NaCl in
animal-specific order. E22's three-butanone/adaptation claims are invalid. E23's
sampler mechanics remain historical evidence, but its pooled biological clock was
wrong. E24 corrects those definitions. E27 uses binary-any-stimulus conditioning;
chemical labels are post-sampling strata. E32 supersedes E28's negative point-score
conclusion after longer training, but does not repair historical80 provenance.

Original `results/` paths are unavailable. The retained code, model-development PDF,
September synthesis `src/data.json`, dashboards' validation documents and experiment
index are evidence of reported outcomes, not a replacement for missing run arrays.
Other result directories, including SBTG and history-tangent results, are present.

## Fresh replication

30 primary fits; 120 progressive N64 archives; 40 independent MC repeats; 40 direct
N4096 comparisons; 60 endpoint-timing archives; 1,440 diagnostics; 30 predictive
evaluations. Source, data, receipts and outputs are preserved. The incomplete
extension (9/15 fits, 34/60 archives) is excluded from the primary ensemble.

The final `REPORT_FINAL.md` supersedes the interpretation in `REPORT_STOPPED.md`.
The historical README/status descriptions must not be used to infer current activity.
No new NeuroPAL fits or paths are generated by the publication analysis.

## Synthetic collections

- September generator tradeoffs: nine families, four laws, three data seeds, two
  neural initialization seeds, 192 completed tasks. Three-input/three-output laws;
  conditional effects and history derivatives, not a full NeuroPAL path-lag matrix.
- Fresh Gaussian path oracle: independent check of the repaired-path law and
  finite-particle estimator on a known linear system.
- History-tangent benchmark: convergence, finite contrasts, diffusion/AR/flow/ratio
  comparisons, derivative-readiness and known-law failures.
- July adjudication: 250 objective-matched cases and classical controls.
- Supporting distributional/empirical SID and neuromodulation programs: preserved
  separately as methodological background; they are not new NeuroPAL replications.
- Original OOD program: code and embedded summary tables remain; raw outputs missing.
